# Code of Conduct

MBUDDY is a project about making a small, friendly companion. The people who work on it should be treated the same way.

## Our pledge

We want everyone to feel welcome here, whatever their age, background, identity, experience, language or ability. We pledge to
make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and explain things without making someone feel small.
- Give and take feedback gracefully: criticise the work, not the person.
- Say what you tried and what you saw, so others can help. A question that shows its effort is always welcome.
- Respect other people's time, privacy and boundaries. Never share someone's personal information.
- Keep the project's own promises: nothing that surprises or harms the people who use it (see CONTRIBUTING.md).

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning comments, in any form and in any place connected to the project.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated pressure after someone has said no.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat, events) and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful. They will say why.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private "Report content" on the item, or contact a maintainer listed in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) directly. Reports are handled privately, and the person who reports is protected from retaliation.

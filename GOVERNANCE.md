# How the project is run

MBUDDY is a small project that wants to grow into a community. This page says who decides what, so that anyone can see how a
change gets in and how someone becomes a regular.

## Maintainers

| Who | Role |
|---|---|
| [@bitalchemistio-004](https://github.com/bitalchemistio-004) | Founder and lead maintainer: final say on direction, the licence, and releases |

More maintainers will be added here as people earn trust (see below).

## How decisions are made

- **Everyday changes** (a fix, a doc, a small feature): one maintainer approval on the pull request is enough.
- **Bigger changes** (a new part, a change to what MBUDDY is allowed to do on someone's phone or PC, anything touching privacy or
  safety): opened first as an issue or a short proposal, discussed in the open, and agreed by the lead maintainer. The rules in
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#the-ground-rules) are the test.
- **Safety and privacy changes** (the refused-app list, what counts as a yes, anything sent off the device) always need a test and
  a clear note of what changes for the person using it.
- When people disagree, the maintainers decide, and say why.

## How to become a maintainer

Make several good contributions over time, review other people's pull requests helpfully, and follow the code of conduct. A
maintainer will invite you; there is no exam and no minimum number.

## Releases

Maintainers cut a release when the tests pass on the main branch and the changelog is updated. Version numbers rise with every
build (see [docs/BUILD.md](docs/BUILD.md)); the changelog says what changed in words a user can follow.

## The record

Why something is the way it is lives in the pull request and issue that changed it, and in the
[changelog](CHANGELOG.md). Link the issue in every pull request.
